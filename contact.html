§ A useful first message

You don’t need a polished brief. A little context and a clear question are enough.

For projects
The problem, the current tools and what a useful outcome would look like.
For research
The question, paper or opportunity you’d like to discuss.
For opportunities
The role or collaboration, and the timing.

Please leave out passwords, private records and other sensitive information.
