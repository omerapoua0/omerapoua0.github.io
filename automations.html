01From my work · BP, 2024

A weekly report, without a weekly rebuild.

At BP I built a competitor intelligence engine for the offshore-wind market intelligence team. The useful part was connecting the steps into a process the team could run again.

03Approach

Make the hand-offs explicit.

A useful automation has a clear input, a checkable output and a plan for when something goes wrong.

  1. 01

    Map the existing process

    What triggers it? Which tools and people are involved? Where does the time disappear?

  2. 02

    Keep control where it matters

    Define permissions, review points and what should stop for a human decision.

  3. 03

    Test the awkward cases

    Missing data, failed requests and uncertain results deserve attention before a workflow becomes routine.
