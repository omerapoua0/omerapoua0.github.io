    04Questions

    Before you enquire.

    Which subjects and levels can I enquire about?

    Maths and science at GCSE Foundation, GCSE Higher and A-level. Tell me your exam board and topics so we can confirm a good fit on the intro call.

    How much do lessons cost?

    Lesson length, pricing and availability are agreed directly once we’ve discussed what you need. No payment is taken on this website.

    Is sending the form a booking?

    No. The form prepares an email that you send yourself. I reply within one working day, we have a free 15-minute intro call, and then agree times together.

    How do you keep under-18s safe?

    I hold an enhanced DBS check. Lessons for under-18s are arranged with a parent or guardian, who is welcome to sit in on any lesson.

    Who will be teaching?

    Me. I’m a Mathematics & Physics Teaching Assistant at Southfields Academy, teaching GCSE Higher maths and physics, and I study Computer Science & Mathematics at Birkbeck.
