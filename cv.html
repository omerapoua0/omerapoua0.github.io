01Profile

Understand it. Build it. Explain it.

I build machine-learning and optimisation systems from the mathematics through to production code.

I’m strong in Python, SQL and statistical modelling, with practical experience in Bayesian inference, predictive modelling and data pipelines. My work spans network autonomy, predictive modelling, market intelligence and learning products.

Engineering and teaching ask me to do something similar: find the structure inside a difficult problem and make the next step clearer. Longer term, I want to pursue deeper research in optimisation and intelligent systems.
