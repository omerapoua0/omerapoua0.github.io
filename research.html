01The notebook

Five questions, honestly labelled.

Each card says what the question is, why it matters to me and exactly how far along it is.

Q1

AI & higher education

In progress · 2026 – present · unpublished

Q1/05

How efficiently do AI models complete university assignments, and what does that do to learning?

Completing a task and learning from it are different outcomes.

Independent research investigating how efficiently AI models complete university assignments and the effect on student learning outcomes.

Teaching GCSE maths and physics, and building NOOKBASE, give me two more perspectives on the same question: how to make understanding part of the product, not an afterthought.

Independent research · AI evaluation · Learning outcomes

The product direction: NOOKBASE ↗
Q2

Mathematical optimisation

Incoming · Summer 2027

Q2/05

How do you choose a useful action when resources, information and time are limited?

Better decisions under real constraints.

My interests connect optimisation with machine learning and decision-making, alongside coursework in linear algebra, calculus and discrete mathematics.

I have been selected for a Santander Fintech Research Placement in summer 2027, applying mathematical optimisation and machine learning to credit-scoring models. It is upcoming, not completed research.

Optimisation · Machine learning · Credit scoring

Q3

Quantitative modelling

Exploratory

Q3/05

What is a forecasting model actually learning, and how do you know?

A good prediction is only interesting if we understand how it was tested.

I am developing my knowledge of probability, stochastic processes and time-series methods. Building crypto prediction models at Bitget made the distinction between signal and noise a practical question.

Research and modelling interests only. Nothing here is investment advice or a claim of trading performance.

Probability · Stochastic processes · Time series

The Bitget modelling project ↗
Q4

Quantum computing

Independent study

Q4/05

What does computation look like when linear algebra becomes the machine?

Linear algebra becomes something you can build and experiment with.

I am exploring quantum computing with Qiskit, connecting mathematical foundations with small computational experiments. This is a learning direction, not original published quantum research.

Qiskit · Linear algebra · Independent study

Research, with depth.

Intelligent systems · Optimisation · Quantitative methods
